Gate Not Working?

Common faults and
what to check first.

These first checks are safe to do yourself. Do not open the control board or touch 220 V wiring; that work needs a technician.

The motor does not respond

Check the fuse box and whether the LEDs on the motor are lit. If they are completely dark, the control board or power supply may have failed.

Remote fails, wall button works

Replace the remote battery first. If it still fails, the remote may need to be re-programmed into the receiver.

The gate stops halfway

Look for stones, leaves, or debris on the track or in the gate path. Travel-limit settings may also have shifted and need adjusting.

The motor runs but the gate does not move

A worn or broken gear is possible. Switch the motor off and do not force it; this needs a parts replacement.

The photocell light keeps flashing

Wipe the photocell lenses with a soft cloth and check that the facing pair is still aligned. Direct sun can also confuse the sensor.

The gate will not open in a power cut

Use the manual release key to open the gate by hand. A backup battery can be added so the gate keeps working during outages.

If the problem continues, or you notice a burning smell or a damaged board, disconnect the power and message us with the motor model and a short description. Our detailed fault guide is currently in Turkish: Arıza Rehberi.

Remotes

Lost or broken remote?

Most modern gate remotes use rolling code: the code changes with every press, so a remote cannot simply be copied. A compatible new remote is programmed into the motor's receiver instead, and a lost remote can be deleted from its memory so it can no longer open the gate. On many receivers the memory is cleared in one go, so please have all the remotes you want to keep with you on the day.

To help us identify the right remote, send photos of the front and back of the remote and of the label on the motor or control board. More detail is in our Turkish guide: Kumanda ve Rolling Code.

Frequently Asked

Do you speak English?

Yes. We communicate by phone, WhatsApp, or email in English throughout the quote and repair process.

Is the on-site survey free?

Yes, within the Bodrum peninsula. We measure your gate, assess the site, and send a written quote at no charge.

Can you repair or service a gate motor installed by another company?

In most cases, yes. Send us a photo of the motor or control board label with the brand and model, and we will confirm compatibility before the visit. If a spare part is hard to source, we will tell you before you commit.

My remote is lost or broken. Can you replace it?

Usually yes. Most modern gate remotes use rolling code, so they cannot simply be copied. Instead, a compatible new remote is programmed into the receiver and the lost remote is deleted from its memory. Send photos of the remote and of the motor or control board label so we can confirm the right remote family.

Can I open my gate from my smartphone?

Yes, with Control X Access. It works with automatic gate motors that have a button (dry-contact) input, and compatibility is checked during the survey. Your existing remotes and manual release keep working. App availability for Android and iPhone is changing, so ask us for the current status.

What warranty do you offer?

All genuine Nice motors carry a 2-year distributor warranty covering manufacturing defects.
